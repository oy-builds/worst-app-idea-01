# The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched

A make.fun entry for "The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched": one sentence
pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable. FridgeLight pings you every
time your fridge light turns on and sends a weekly digest of your leftovers'
hours in the dark — because your food deserves an audience and you deserve to
know. A product that sounds real, a founder who sounds certain, a function
nobody on earth needs.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the pitch to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`):

> FridgeLight is the app that pings you every time your fridge light turns on and sends a weekly digest of your leftovers' hours in the dark, because your food deserves an audience and you deserve to know.

Run it with `node main.ts`.
